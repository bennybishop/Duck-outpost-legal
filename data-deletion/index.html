Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Legal home Privacy Policy Data Deletion

Duck Outpost does not currently provide user accounts or Facebook Login. Most game progress and preferences are stored locally on your Android device. Firebase Analytics and Meta App Events may process pseudonymous analytics and attribution data as described in the Privacy Policy.

Delete data stored on your device

You can delete local Duck Outpost data at any time by using one of these methods:

  • Use the game’s Reset / Start over option to reset game progress.
  • Open Android Settings → Apps → Duck Outpost → Storage → Clear storage. Menu names can differ by Android version.
  • Uninstall Duck Outpost. Android backup or restore features may preserve or later restore some local data, depending on your device settings.

Request deletion or assistance

Email benjamin.dehant@gmail.com with the subject Duck Outpost Data Deletion Request.

In the message, include:

  • that your request concerns Duck Outpost for Android;
  • the country where you use the game;
  • the approximate installation and activity dates; and
  • the type of request, such as deletion, access, objection, or restriction.

Do not send passwords, private keys, payment details, or copies of identity documents unless we later explain why specific verification is legally necessary.

What happens next

We will acknowledge and review your request and may ask for limited additional technical details needed to determine whether a relevant record can be located. We aim to respond within 30 days, or within the period required by applicable law.

Because the game has no account or login, your email address may not be linked to the pseudonymous identifiers used by Firebase Analytics or Meta App Events. This can make it impossible to identify a particular analytics record from an email alone. We will not claim to have deleted data that we cannot reliably locate.

Where a record can be reasonably identified and deletion is applicable, we will process the request or forward the appropriate request through the relevant service-provider process. Some aggregated, de-identified, security, or legally required records may be retained where permitted by law.
